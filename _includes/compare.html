{%- comment -%} Comparison slider: two 1:1 crops of the same frame region on two presets (or two settings), one over the other, with a divider (assets/js/compare.js). Both renders come from _data/renders.yml, must have the same pixel size and crop position, and follow the crop rules of _includes/crop.html (one file shown 1:1 at every pixel ratio, optional HDR AVIF). The upper render sits in a wrapper with overflow: hidden whose width is the divider position, a whole number of device pixels; there is no canvas and no clip-path. Without JavaScript the two renders appear one after the other, each with its label.
